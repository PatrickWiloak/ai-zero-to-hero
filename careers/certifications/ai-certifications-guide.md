# AI Certifications Guide

**In one line:** An index of the AI and machine learning certifications that are still available as of 2026-10 (Anthropic, AWS, Google Cloud, NVIDIA, Databricks, Oracle, GitHub), which ones fit which role, which were retired in 2026, and links to a free study guide for each on the sibling Zero to Hero site.
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-21

---

## The short version

- Certifications are most useful when you **work with a specific vendor or cloud**, sell or advise on it (solutions, consulting, pre-sales), or need structure for self-study. For hands-on AI engineering roles, **projects matter more**; see the [AI engineer roadmap](../roles/ai-engineer-roadmap.md).
- **Start with one foundational exam** that matches your stack, then one associate or professional exam aligned with your target role. Two well-chosen certifications beat six random ones.
- **Several popular AI exams were retired in 2026**: AWS Machine Learning Engineer - Associate (MLA-C01) and Microsoft's AI-900, AI-102 and DP-100. Older roadmaps still recommend them. Check the retired list below before you study.
- Every certification below links to a free study guide (notes, practice plans, flashcards) on [Cloud, Data, AI and Security Zero to Hero](https://patrickwiloak.github.io/cloud-data-ai-security-zero-to-hero/), this site's sibling.
- Prices and formats change. Figures here come from the Zero to Hero guides, which were last updated between May and October 2026; confirm on the vendor's page before booking.

## Which certification for which goal

| Your goal | Good first pick | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Prove AI literacy for a non-technical or business role | Google Cloud Generative AI Leader, Claude Certified Associate - Foundations, or AWS AI Practitioner | Role-specific vendor training |
| Build LLM apps on Claude | Claude Certified Developer - Foundations | Claude Certified Architect - Foundations, then Professional |
| Build GenAI on AWS | AWS AI Practitioner | AWS Generative AI Developer - Professional |
| ML engineering on Google Cloud | Google Cloud Generative AI Leader (optional) | Professional Machine Learning Engineer |
| GenAI and ML on Databricks | Databricks Generative AI Engineer Associate or ML Associate | ML Professional |
| Vendor-neutral GenAI and GPU knowledge | NVIDIA Generative AI LLMs Associate | NVIDIA Generative AI LLMs Professional or Agentic AI Professional |
| AI infrastructure and GPU operations | NVIDIA AI Infrastructure and Operations Associate | NVIDIA AI Infrastructure Professional or AI Operations Professional |
| Oracle Cloud shop | OCI AI Foundations | OCI Generative AI Professional |
| Developer productivity with AI | GitHub Copilot | - |

## How to prepare efficiently

1. **Read the official exam guide** for domain weights. Spend time in proportion.
2. **Do practice exams early** to find weak domains, then study those. Cheat sheets and fact sheets help for last-week review.
3. **Get hands-on** with the vendor's console or API. Scenario questions reward people who have actually built something. The projects in [build/](../../build/projects/chatbot-in-an-afternoon.md) double as exam preparation for the developer-level exams.
4. **Learn the concepts once**: most AI exams test the same fundamentals (RAG, embeddings, fine-tuning vs prompting, evaluation, responsible AI). Start with [RAG explained](../../learn/concepts/rag-explained.md), [fine-tuning vs RAG](../../learn/concepts/fine-tuning-vs-rag.md), [evals for LLMs](../../learn/concepts/evals-for-llms.md) and [responsible AI basics](../../safety/governance/responsible-ai-basics.md).
5. **Book the exam** before you feel ready; a date focuses study.

## Are certifications worth it?

- **Yes** for consulting, pre-sales, solutions architecture and partner roles, where vendors and clients count them; for structured learning; and for getting past CV filters in some companies.
- **Less so** for research roles and for hands-on AI engineering at product companies, where interviewers ask about what you built. See [AI engineer interview](../interviews/ai-engineer-interview.md).
- **Watch renewal costs**: some AI certifications are valid for only 12 months (Anthropic's) or two years (NVIDIA's, Databricks'), because the field moves fast.

## Common mistakes

- **Studying for a retired exam** from an old roadmap.
- **Collecting certifications instead of building.** After two, a project adds more.
- **Choosing by popularity** rather than the stack you use or want to work with.
- **Memorising dumps.** Scenario questions punish it, and it breaks exam agreements.
