# NVIDIA-Certified Professional - AI Networking (NCP-AIN)

## Exam Overview

Validates deploying, configuring, managing and troubleshooting NVIDIA networking for AI data centers: Spectrum Ethernet, InfiniBand, Kubernetes integration and automation. NVIDIA's code for this exam is **NCP-AIN**; this guide called it "NCP-NET" until 2026-10-22.

**Exam code:** NCP-AIN  
**Duration:** 2 hours  
**Questions:** 70-75  
**Cost:** $400 USD; NVIDIA says lab exercises are being added on November 1 (2026), when the price rises to $500  
**Level:** Professional  
**Validity:** 2 years from issuance  
**Prerequisites:** 2-3 years of hands-on data center networking experience with NVIDIA hardware (recommended)  
**Passing score:** Not published
